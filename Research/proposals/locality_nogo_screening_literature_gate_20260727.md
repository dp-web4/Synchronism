**Q2 — two branches:**

## EXECUTED — verdicts (2026-07-27, same session)

Full writeup: `synchronism-site/explorer/findings/locality-nogo-screening-walk-counterexample-found.md`

**Q1 — the registered branch set was incomplete.** Branches 1a/1b/1c all presupposed the literature
could only contain a *precedent*. It contains a **counterexample**: Burrage, Copeland & Millington,
PRD 95, 064050 (2017) reproduce the RAR on SPARC-153 with a scalar keyed on local volumetric ρ(r)
and universal Lagrangian parameters. The no-go's universal-quantifier form ("holds for any local-ρ
MOND mimic") is **false**. Two site statements refuted: the unqualified "None exists" (07-23) and
"the ρ-vs-g_bar mismatch is the standard reason screened scalars don't reproduce MOND" (07-10, an
unsourced and inverted attribution). On the axis the registration did cover, **1a fires**: the
derivative-order screening taxonomy (Joyce, Jain, Khoury & Trodden, Phys. Rep. 568, 1 (2015)) is a
finer version of the site's locality-triage lemma.

**Q2 — 2a on mathematical form AND on physical job.** The symmetron has the action, the derived
ρ_* = μ²M², the genuine Z₂ order parameter, and the mean-field exponent β = ½ that the framework
claimed for C(ρ) and audited itself out of — and was pointed at the RAR in 2017.

**Q3 — NO CATCH.** The blind vocabulary pass missed a target that was present and findable. Root
cause: the detector searches for the *claim* ("show me that X fails"), which retrieves confirmations,
never counterexamples — confirmation-seeking by construction. Successor design: search for the
**object** (construction, dataset, relation), not the claim. The 4/4 stays corpus-specific.

**Falsifier check:** the primary-source requirement was met — Hinterbichler & Khoury (1001.4525),
Joyce et al. (1407.0059), and Burrage et al. (1610.07529) were all read via full-text HTML, not
abstracts or secondaries. The session's conclusion is registered as executed.

**Gate: NOT CLEARED.** Transferable null #1 cannot ship in its current form.

**Two methodology rules proposed, both generalizing beyond this case:**
1. A prior-art null must be stated **scoped to its corpus** in the same sentence ("none in the MOND
   variable-comparison corpus"), never as an unqualified "none exists." A corpus-scoped audit finds
   prior art only where it already expects it.
2. Every attribution of a position to an external literature ("standard," "the usual reason," "well
   known in X") must name a primary source that states it, or be deleted. The existing guard —
   every p-value walks to a primary file — does not catch **fabricated consensus**, because a
   consensus claim has no walkable target.
