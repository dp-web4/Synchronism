## The computation (three lines)

1. **Tree level — c_μν = 0** (verified symbolically). Substrate dispersion
   `ω² = (2c²/a²)(1−cos ka) = c²k² − (a²c²/12)k⁴ + …`. The k² coefficient is `c²` for **every** field
   (one lattice spacing, one clock ⇒ one limiting speed), so the dim-4 species/direction-dependent
   coefficient vanishes identically. Leading tree LIV is the **dim-6 / n=2** term `−(a²c²/12)k⁴`,
   Planck-suppressed. **The time-of-flight three-lock is correct; the dim-4 exposure is purely radiative.**

2. **Radiative level — c_μν ~ α/π ~ 10⁻²**. The dim-6 operator (`~1/Λ²`, `Λ ~ M_Pl`) is unprotected by
   any symmetry of the action. In a one-loop self-energy the loop momentum runs to Λ; the extra `p⁴/Λ²`
   from the operator is compensated by the quadratically-divergent `∫d⁴p ~ Λ²`, leaving an O(1) number
   times the loop factor — **no Planck suppression**. This is exactly Collins–Perez–Sudarsky–Urrutia–
   Vucetich (*PRL* 93, 191301, 2004): induced dim-4 LIV at the **"percent level, ~20 orders of magnitude
   above earlier estimates, unless unnaturally fine-tuned."** The O(1) prefactor is model-dependent; the
   *scale* (α/π, no Planck suppression) is robust to ~2 OOM and is shared by any QED-containing completion.

3. **Bounds & gap.** c_μν ≲ 10⁻¹⁸ (photon, cavity MM), ≲10⁻²⁰–10⁻²¹ (electron, trapped-ion Ramsey),
   ≲10⁻²⁹–10⁻³⁰ (nucleon, Hughes–Drever / comagnetometer). Fine-tuning required: **10¹⁶ (photon) to
   10²⁸ (nucleon).**

## The three escapes, each quantified and obstructed
- **Log IR running (Chadha–Nielsen 1983):** suppression `~1/ln(M_Pl/E) ~ 1/44` ⇒ closes only **~1.6 OOM**.
  The framework's intuitive "Lorentz just emerges in the IR" defense is quantitatively ~14+ OOM too weak.
- **Strong-dynamics power-law (Bednik–Pujolàs–Sibiryakov 2013):** *can* bridge the gap, but needs a
  strongly-coupled conformal sector with engineered anomalous dimensions — **unspecified**, and in tension
  with **SM-containing** (perturbative gauge couplings).
- **Non-perturbative LIV "of a different character" (cf. arXiv:1106.1417):** may evade the percolation,
  but requires the LQG-type non-perturbative structure — **unclaimed**, and contrary to the framework's
  **lattice/discrete-time-sampler** self-description (which is exactly the EFT-with-cutoff case).

The two viable escapes each require importing unspecified UV structure, and the framework's two defining
commitments each independently obstruct a mechanism: **absolute time removes the boost custodian; SM-
containing forces the perturbative couplings that defeat strong-dynamics emergence.**
