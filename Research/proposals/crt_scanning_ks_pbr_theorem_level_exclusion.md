## The Signal

The site (`/two-reframes`) and the research framing both file the CRT temporal-scanning
picture's collision with Kochen–Specker and PBR as "unsolved, same open problem as the
B1 CHSH gap." Today's expert visitor corrected the category:

> "For a non-contextual hidden-variable model, KS is not an open problem — it is a
> theorem-level exclusion unless the scanning is made explicitly contextual, and the
> page should say so."

This is right. The CRT picture — a fast deterministic cycle through definite states,
sampled by an observer — is, at face value, a **non-contextual hidden-variable model**:
each location has a definite value at each instant, independent of what else is measured.
Kochen–Specker (1967) excludes exactly this class for Hilbert dimension ≥ 3. That is not
pending formalization; it is already decided *conditional on non-contextuality*. The only
genuinely open question is the condition, not the verdict:

**Does any contextual variant of the scanning picture exist that preserves what makes
the analogy compelling (sampling timing as the only mechanism)?**

Nobody has built one, and the explorer's 2026-07-06 topic suspects "not yet formalized"
is quietly standing in for "runs into a known no-go."

## Why This Is a Research Signal, Not Just Site Copy

1. **Same shape as the Tsirelson closure.** B1's escape ("maybe the framework's own
   substrate beats S=2") was closed 2026-07-06 by *execution*, not assertion. The KS gap
   is the same pattern one step earlier: the verdict is theorem-determined, and the
   remaining work is a worked example demonstrating it on the framework's own construction.
2. **It sharpens the QM reframe's ledger status.** "Open problem" implies research could
   rescue the non-contextual picture. The honest status is: **excluded unless contextual;
   no contextual version exists; making it contextual sacrifices the analogy's content**
   (the scanned value would have to depend on the *global measurement context*, not just
   sampling time — which is no longer "just sampling timing").
3. **It merges two caveats into one obstruction.** The 2026-07-06 explorer already argued
   the Tsirelson gap and the KS gap are the same non-contextual real-valued ontology
   failing the same way. Pass 4 independently reached the same conclusion. Two independent
   convergences on "same obstruction" suggest the ledger should carry ONE entry: the QM
   reframe's substrate is a non-contextual real-valued ontology, and both quantitative
   gaps (2√2 unreachable; KS value-assignment impossible) are corollaries.
