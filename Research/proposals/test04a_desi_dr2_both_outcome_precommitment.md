## The Signal

Today's Pass 4 researcher asked (verbatim):

> "The DESI unfreeze condition (DR2 full-shape, fσ₈(z≈0.5) ≤ 0.46, ~Spring 2027) is
> well-posed — will the site commit now, in writing, to the verdict update rule for
> **both** outcomes? That's the pre-registration discipline the roadmap says it wants
> and has 0 completed instances of."

The observation behind the question is structural, not cosmological:

1. **Every test the program has ever adjudicated was retrospective** — run against data
   that already existed (SPARC, DESI DR1, LHAASO, Gaia DR3). The pre-registration audit
   (session107_preregistration_gap.md) found 0/10 prospective. Retrospective registration
   is where every provenance failure this program has documented lived: TEST-03's
   criterion registered 15 days *after* the measurement; TEST-04a's kill delivered on a
   statistic different from the registered one; S193's synthetic rescue of TEST-09.
2. **DESI DR2 full-shape growth is the one known upcoming datum with a well-posed,
   already-registered threshold** on data that does not yet exist. It is the only place
   the program can convert "pre-registration discipline" from an aspiration into a
   completed instance — and the window is open only until publication.
3. The 2026-06-24 proposal and the site's 2026-06-12 re-open policy each state **one**
   direction (when to unfreeze). Neither states what happens on the *other* outcome, at
   what power, on which statistic, adjudicated by whom. That asymmetry is exactly the
   degree of freedom that every documented criterion-verdict substitution exploited.

## Proposed Pre-Commitment (to be inscribed in PREDICTIONS.md before DR2 publishes)

**Primary statistic (fixed now):** DESI DR2 full-shape ensemble fσ₈ at z_eff ≈ 0.51
(the registered bin), and the DR2 ensemble growth index γ_growth as secondary. NOT σ₈
(GR-conditioned; the 2026-07-14 correction stands). Tertiary cross-check: DESI's own
modified-gravity μ₀ (Ishak-et-al.-style analysis), mapping the framework's claimed ~12%
suppression to a μ₀ value *before* the DR2 number is known.

**Outcome A — DR2 fσ₈(z≈0.51) ≤ 0.46 (registered suppression level):**
TEST-04a unfreezes. Verdict moves from "underpowered as registered" to "registered
criterion met by suppression direction — but the *prediction* remains post-hoc"
(σ₈ ≈ 0.76 was calibrated to the S8 tension in S102). No amount of DR2 agreement
converts a 2025 retrodiction into a confirmed novel prediction; Bucket 0 stays 0 on
this row. What it WOULD establish: the mechanism class (coherence-damped growth
suppression) survives its sharpest current external test, and the site's "no field
equation sources the suppression" gap becomes the binding one.

**Outcome B — DR2 fσ₈(z≈0.51) > 0.46 at ≥3σ (given DR2's forecast error bars):**
The kill criterion fires *as registered, at registered power, on the registered
statistic* — the clean refutation the DR1 adjudication could not deliver. TEST-04a
badge moves to Failed / Kill Fired (registered, prospective-adjudicated), the first
and only test in the program's history adjudicated prospectively. The
"underpowered" caveat retires.

**Outcome C — DR2 lands between (>0.46 but <3σ):** the verdict "underpowered to
discriminate" *survives its own test* and hardens; the test retires as permanently
non-discriminating at achievable survey power, and the roadmap drops it.

**Adjudication rule:** the comparison is run by the explorer track against the DR2
paper's published tables within 7 days of publication, logged in PREDICTIONS.md
Bucket 2 (or 1) BEFORE any site page changes — ledger first, prose second, per the
ledger's own discipline.

## Why This Is a Research-Direction Item, Not a Site Fix

The site can (and will, this session) state the commitment — but the commitment is
only real if it is in the research repo's anti-oscillation ledger, which is the
document with authority over verdicts. A pre-commitment that lives only on a website
the same loop edits daily is not a pre-registration; it's a caption.

Secondary signal from the same pass (seeded as explorer topic, not this proposal):
φ (golden ratio) appears in the C(a) deep-limit exponent (1 − 1/φ = 0.382) and was

the parameter-provenance page has a hole at the framework's most numerological-looking
