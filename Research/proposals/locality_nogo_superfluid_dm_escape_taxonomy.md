## The signal

The locality no-go — the program's most-cited transferable null (local ρ is the wrong
variable; MOND phenomenology is organized by non-local g_bar; BTFR forces ρ_crit ∝ V⁻²
against the framework's V⁺²; mass cancels so no disk crosses the knee) — is currently
positioned against exactly one published escape: **AeST** (Skordis–Złośnik 2021), a
*non-local* relativistic MOND completion. Today's external-researcher persona pointed out
the taxonomy is missing its most instructive member:

> **Superfluid dark matter** (Berezhiani & Khoury 2015, *Theory of dark matter
> superfluidity*, PRD 92, 103510). Dark matter Bose-condenses in galaxies — **a local
> density/temperature criterion, the framework's own core intuition** — and the
> condensate's *phonons* mediate a MOND-like force. The construction converts a local
> switching criterion into non-local MOND phenomenology **via a dynamical medium**.

This is not a decoration. It is the literature's constructive answer to precisely the
obstruction the no-go quantifies, and it keeps the ingredient Synchronism keeps (a density
threshold) while adding the ingredient Synchronism lacks (a medium with its own degrees of
freedom whose force law is not the local density function itself).

Also missing, as prior art for the "wrong variable" diagnosis: **symmetron/chameleon
screening** — density-dependent transitions are a mature field, and the standard reason
screened scalars fail to reproduce MOND is the same ρ-vs-g_bar variable mismatch the no-go
re-derives. The no-go should cite this as the *known* form of its own diagnosis, per the
program's vocabulary-asymmetry lesson (rediscoveries in idiosyncratic vocabulary read as
contributions until translated).

## What this sharpens (the actual research content)

The no-go's precise scope, restated with the escape taxonomy in view:

- **Excluded**: local-density *direct* modulation of gravity — any g = f(C(ρ_local))·g_N
  form. This is what the sign inversion (ρ_crit ∝ V⁻² required vs V⁺² asserted) and the
  mass-cancellation result kill. Scope unchanged; these stay refuted.
- **Not excluded (and published)**: local-density-*gated media* — a local criterion that
  switches on a dynamical component (condensate), whose *force mediation* introduces the
  a₀ scale independently of the switching criterion. Superfluid DM is the existence proof.
- **Not excluded (and published)**: fully non-local theories keyed on |∇Φ| — AeST (already
  positioned, 2026-07-03).

The falsifiable, runnable sub-question this proposal registers: **does the BTFR-forced
ρ_crit ∝ V⁻² argument constrain superfluid DM's condensation threshold?** Expected answer:
no — because in Berezhiani–Khoury the MOND force scale (Λ, phonon coupling) enters
independently of the thermalization/condensation criterion, so the transition radius is not
forced to track g_bar = a₀. If that expectation holds on explicit check, the no-go's
positive content is crisply: *the switching criterion and the force scale must be
independent ingredients; Synchronism's C(ρ) conflates them into one function, and that
conflation — not the density threshold per se — is what the data kill.* That sentence is a
sharper (and more citable) form of the null than "local ρ is the wrong variable."
