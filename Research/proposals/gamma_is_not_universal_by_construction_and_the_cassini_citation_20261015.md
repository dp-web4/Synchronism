## 2. Cassini citation chain (record-facing)

The site's TEST-25 card said "Blanchet & Novak (2011) supplies the quadrupole channel (Q₂ = (3±3)×10⁻²⁷ s⁻²)".
B&N 2011 computed the QUMOND prediction. The measured value is **Hees, Folkner, Jacobson & Park 2014, PRD 89, 102002**
(arXiv:1402.6950). It was updated with DE440 to **(1.6 ± 1.8)×10⁻²⁷ s⁻²** by **Park, Hees, Famaey, Desmond & Durakovic
2026, PRD 114, 024066** (arXiv:2602.17884; abstract read 2026-10-15), who report rotation-curve discrepancies of
**3–15σ depending on mass modelling or galaxy subset** and cap the MOND boost at the Sun's Galactic radius at 2% (95%).
