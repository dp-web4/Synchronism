## The proposed registration (one paragraph, PREDICTIONS.md-adjacent)

**Synchronism has no dark-energy sector.** C(ρ) modulates the effective gravitational
response to *matter* density; nothing in the framework sources, modifies, or couples to the
expansion history. There is no mechanism by which coherence dynamics produce w ≠ −1, w(z)
evolution, or any deviation from whatever background cosmology is assumed. Therefore:

- If DESI DR2+ confirms evolving dark energy, Synchronism inherits the result passively,
  exactly as ΛCDM-with-a-patch would — it neither predicted nor forbids it.
- This is a **scope negative**: the framework's claimed cosmological machinery
  (density→coherence→growth) turns out to touch only the growth side, and its one growth
  number (σ₈ ≈ 0.76) is post-hoc (S102) and disfavored (2.4σ on the GR-conditioned
  amplitude; underpowered on the registered fσ₈ statistic).
- Registering the silence *now* prevents a future retro-fit: any later claim that coherence
  "explains" w₀wₐ should be judged against this dated statement that no such coupling
  exists in the framework as specified.

## Why this is worth a proposal rather than just a site edit

The site edit shipped today (2026-07-22, /honest-assessment cosmology-silence list). But the
site list is downstream; the research repo is where scope statements bind. The pattern this
guards against is the one the archive has already exhibited three times (S193 BTFR rescue,
TEST-03 late threshold, TEST-04a statistic swap): when new data arrives, the absence of a
prior scope statement is what makes retroactive accommodation possible. A dated "no coupling
to w(z) exists" is cheap insurance of the same kind as the DR2 three-branch pre-commitment
(adopted 2026-07-17).
