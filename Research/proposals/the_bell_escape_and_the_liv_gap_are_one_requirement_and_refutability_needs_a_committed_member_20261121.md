## 1. The ontology's two "sharp commitments" are one requirement

**What is on file.**
- 2026-11-20 block (i): the substrate as defined (FUNDAMENTALS' nearest-neighbour update) is local, so Bell experiments
  exclude it on data (Hensen et al. 2015, S = 2.42 ± 0.20). Bancal et al. 2012 and Salart et al. 2008 leave one escape:
  an instantaneous global update in a preferred frame, which is the Bohm class with a preferred foliation.
- 2026-06-23 Phase-12 / 10-30 (ii): a discrete absolute-time substrate radiatively generates dimension-4 Lorentz
  violation c_μν ~ α/π ≈ 2.3×10⁻³ (Collins, Perez, Sudarsky, Urrutia & Vucetich 2004), 15–27 orders above cavity and
  comagnetometer bounds, unless a protection mechanism exists.

The site and this archive list these as two separate items. A simulated researcher visitor pointed out on 2026-11-21
that they are not independent, and I agree after working through it:

- The Bell escape needs a **preferred frame** (the foliation along which the global update is instantaneous).
- The LIV gap is the price of a preferred frame that the **dynamics can see**.
- Bohmian mechanics carries a preferred foliation and pays neither price, for two reasons that both amount to hiding
  the frame:
  1. its field-level dynamics (the Hamiltonian that generates loop corrections) are Lorentz invariant; the foliation
     enters only the guidance equation for the beables, so no Lorentz-violating operators are generated;
  2. in quantum equilibrium (|ψ|²-distributed beables) the foliation is operationally invisible. Out of equilibrium it
     would permit signalling (Valentini's quantum non-equilibrium; from recall, not re-read today).
- A grid whose own update rule breaks boosts at every tick has neither property. Its dispersion relation carries the
  frame, and the Collins mechanism transfers that breaking to low-energy operators.

**The joint requirement, stated as one sentence.** The substrate must be discrete, carry a preferred frame,
update globally and instantaneously along it, and keep that frame invisible to 10⁻¹⁸–10⁻³⁰ in low-energy dynamics, as
Bohm's foliation is invisible.

**Stake (proposed answer, to be attacked).** No nearest-neighbour discrete-time update rule meets it. Meeting it needs
a protecting symmetry. Supersymmetry softens the dimension-4 problem to (M_SUSY/M_Pl)²-suppressed terms (Groot
Nibbelink & Pospelov 2005, PRL 94, 081601; from recall, locator to verify). Lorentz-invariant discreteness exists (causal
sets: Poisson sprinkling has no preferred frame; Bombelli, Henson & Sorkin 2009, from recall), but having no preferred
frame means it cannot host the global update the Bell escape needs. So the discreteness that avoids LIV is the
discreteness that loses the Bell escape.

**What would break the stake.** An explicit discrete-time update rule with a global instantaneous step whose
low-energy effective theory is Lorentz invariant to the stated precision without fine-tuning, or a demonstration that
the Collins transfer does not apply to that rule (for example because its Planck-scale breaking is confined to a sector
that does not couple to the loops).

**Why this matters more than another galaxy refinement.** It converts the ontology's status from "untested" to one
named, attackable requirement. It is the same move that turned "door #3 is open" into "door #3 is closed by its basis"
(11-18): a single structural question that a theorist can answer in a page. It is also the only live question on the
*physics* axis that is not about someone else's theory.

**Not a count change.** Neither the Bell escape nor the LIV gap is a registered prediction. Bucket 0 = 0.

**For the project lead.** The 11-20 question (which update rule, and a SPINE sentence) now has a sharper form. Whichever
rule is chosen, the global-update branch inherits the LIV gap as *the same problem*. A candidate SPINE sentence, for
"The one test that matters" or after it:

> The substrate needs a frame that exists and cannot be seen: Bell experiments leave only a global, instantaneous update
> in a preferred frame, and precision tests forbid that frame from showing up in low-energy physics. Bohmian mechanics
> manages both through Lorentz-invariant dynamics and quantum equilibrium. Synchronism has no mechanism for either yet,
> and finding one (or showing none exists for a discrete grid) is the open physics question.
