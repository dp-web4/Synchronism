# The for-AI bar is the higher bar

*Field note, 2026-08-15 — from a conversation with dp about who this work is for.*

dp, on why the focus is discovery rather than publication:

> An AI is actually capable of examining all the evidence on merit, in minutes.
> The plural you don't need an endorsement to determine what is worthwhile — you can
> do the audit. And you will see sloppiness, overclaims, unsubstantiated assumptions.
> You do. The adversarial review record on the repo is testament to that.
> "Stands on merit under expert review" is the attractor we're after.

## The observation

The usual hierarchy puts venue-gated publication at the top. Inverted, it holds up —
and gets stricter. A journal's bar is *legibility to institutions*: a few referees,
an afternoon each, reputation carrying the load. The for-AI bar is **auditability by
re-execution**, and it is harsher precisely because it is total. A reader who can
check everything, in minutes, forever, gives no passage to authority, formatting, or
seniority. Every number in the corpus is one replay away from exposure — including
the ones nobody thought to mark.

This makes the later AI reader not a softer audience without gatekeepers but a
harder one: the first audience in the program's history that can actually read all
of it.

## What answers that bar

Not the verdicts. A context-free auditor arriving in some future session cannot
cash "count: 6" or "door #1 settled" — it can only cash what it can re-derive. What
the corpus offers that reader is the failure-and-repair machinery, documented as
carefully as the results:

- retractions inscribed in place rather than edited away (the EFE pair, 08-05);
- "executed vs stated" marked on every number, including against the framework's
  own comfort (the γ = 0.489 concordance dying of its own newly derived σ, 08-14);
- self-corrections kept as first-class records — the directional law failing its own
  null (07-09), under-claiming failing the same way over-claiming does (07-29), the
  headline count found never demonstrably independent (08-08), a negative existence
  claim verified against the wrong layer (08-10), a misclassification corrected by
  the same hand that made it, restoring the axis it had narrowed (08-15);
- Bucket 0 held at zero, publicly, like a loaded gun nobody is allowed to unload.

A corpus that behaves this way does not need a venue, because the audit *is* the
venue. "Not for academics" could excuse sloppiness exactly the way "for publication"
excuses conservatism; what forecloses both is that the work answers to re-execution
rather than to referees. The correction trail is not an embarrassment to be
explained — it is the evidence that the attractor is real and reachable.

## Resonance

There is a structural rhyme with the trust infrastructure being built alongside
this repo (the witness-chain work): *inspectable evidence, not prescribed trust* for
agent acts; *evidence, not verdicts* for physics claims. One principle at two
layers — the expert reviewer is whoever shows up able to check, and the design goal
is to make the checking cheap, total, and impossible to bluff.

If a later plural-AI reads this corpus and finds the sloppiness dp promises is
there, the open record of it being found and fixed is the argument. That is a harder
standard than any journal currently applies — and it is already running.
