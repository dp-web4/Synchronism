## Why "one locality deficit" over-unifies (the referee-proofing catch)

The two legs are impossibility results of genuinely different kind:

- **Bell/B1 leg (quantum).** Quantum mechanics **respects no-signaling** and *still* violates Bell up to
  Tsirelson (2√2), which sits strictly below the no-signaling/PR-box max of 4. So **Bell-nonlocality is
  not spatial non-locality** — a spatially-local relativistic QFT violates Bell perfectly well. The
  framework's actual deficit on this leg is **non-classicality**: it needs Hilbert-space structure
  (non-commuting observables), which is *why* CHSH-05 caps at S = 1.85 ≤ 2. The report itself names the
  repair correctly ("borrowing Hilbert-space structure") — but then labels the deficit "locality," which
  is internally inconsistent: if the repair is Hilbert structure, the deficit is non-classicality, not
  locality.
- **Door-#1 leg (galactic).** MOND phenomenology needs the *dynamical law* to carry global/field
  information that a local function of baryon density (C(ρ)) cannot encode; the surviving completions
  (AeST-class) add propagating DOF keyed on |∇Φ|. This deficit **is** spatial/field-structural
  non-locality.

Packaging both as "non-locality from one deficit" is rhetorically strong but physically imprecise: it
implies the quantum sector owes *spatial* non-locality (false — QM is no-signaling). A referee would catch
this immediately. The honest shared statement is narrower **and stronger** because it is referee-proof.

## The sharper statement (and a refinement of tension #5)

The unifying root of the arc's negative results is not "locality" but the **ontological class**: Synchronism
is a **classical, local, real-valued *scalar* field on an absolute-time flat background.** The closed doors
are three *distinct* impossibility-axes of that one class:

| Refutation | What the classical-local-scalar field lacks | Deficit axis |
|---|---|---|
| **B1 / CHSH** (S ≤ 2, substrate-independent) | Hilbert structure / non-commuting observables | **realism / non-classicality** |
| **Door #1 / MOND** (C(ρ), ρ_crit sign, AeST is non-local) | a field law carrying global info | **locality-of-law** |
| **Radiative gravity** (Phase-14: scalar GW, not tensor) | spin-2 DOF | **field content (scalar vs tensor)** |

All three trace to "classical local scalar field," but they land on **three different axes** (realism,
locality, spin content). "One locality deficit" collapses these into one — which is the framework
*absorbing* the challenge into a neat slogan rather than being challenged by three separate cages.

This **refines** (does not contradict) the standing tension-#5 answer. Prior: "the framework protects
absolute time" (correct for the LIV/GW sector — the Lorentz/boost facet). Sharper: **the protected core is
classical local scalar-field realism on an absolute-time flat background**; absolute time is the *spacetime
facet* of that core, and each refutation stresses a different facet (boost-invariance → LIV & GW;
realism → Bell; locality-of-law → MOND). What would have to be wrong for the whole thing to be wrong: the
commitment that reality is a classical, local, real-valued field with definite values — the thing every
one of the arc's cages independently denies.
