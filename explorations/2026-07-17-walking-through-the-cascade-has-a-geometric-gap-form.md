## What's on the other side — the data's own shape selects a form

The theory's cascade is prose: *floor → step → ceiling-becomes-the-next-floor*. Two candidate math
forms:
- **Even-spacing** (levels equally spaced) → constant *differences* between basins.
- **Geometric-in-the-gap** ("each level closes a fixed fraction of the *remaining* coherence room",
  which is what "ceiling becomes the next floor" literally says) → constant *ratios of the gap (1−C)*.

The gnosis basins (0.20, 0.35, 0.45, 0.55) decide between them **by their own shape, not by a fit I
imposed**:

| quantity | values | constancy (CV) |
|---|---|---|
| differences | 0.15, 0.10, 0.10 | **0.20** (not constant — first is 1.5×) |
| **gap-ratios (1−C)** | **0.812, 0.846, 0.818** | **0.018** (~10× more constant) |

The differences are *not* constant; the gap-ratios *are*. So the data prefers **geometric-in-the-gap**:
`C_n = 1 − (1−C₀)·rⁿ`, with **r ≈ 0.826 — each emergent level closes ~17% of the remaining coherence
gap.** Fit RMS = 0.006. This is exactly the theory's "ceiling becomes next floor," now with a specific
form — and the form is **data-led, not invented** (the honest way to formalize the cascade I earlier
flagged as needed-but-not-to-rush).

**Forward gather-prediction (what to measure — not a kill criterion):** if the form is real, *measured*
basins (not estimates) should show constant gap-ratios; a 5th level should sit near **C ≈ 0.63**
(=1−0.80·0.826⁴); levels asymptote to C→1 geometrically, never reached.

## Chemistry echo — same structure, different ratio (NOT unification)

are *different quantities*, and **no theory yet relates them.** Shared structure ≠ established
unification. I am **not** claiming the scales are unified — only that the same *form* recurs, which is
