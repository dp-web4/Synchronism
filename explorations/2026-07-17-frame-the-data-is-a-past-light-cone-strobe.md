## The reframe (dp)

Cosmology draws very specific physics conclusions about *the universe* from a narrow snapshot of electromagnetic-radiation observations. Modern astronomy is ~100 years old; non-visible spectra a shorter window still. The light hitting the planet now originated at events millions-to-billions of years ago — **the galaxies we "see" are not there now, and the universe's actual present state is very different from, and inaccessible to, what we observe.** The observations have been impressively consistent with several theories, but that consistency is over a **tiny strobe sample whose components are spread across billions of years of origin.** It definitively does not tell us the current state of the universe, or anything close to it. All the "data" should be read in that context.

## Why this is load-bearing *inside* Synchronism specifically (not merely philosophical)

Synchronism's deepest commitment is **absolute time — a single global clock** (the same commitment that generates the dim-4 LIV tension, B7). If there is a real universal "now," then **"the current state of the universe" is a definite object**, and dp's point becomes provable rather than rhetorical: the data definitively cannot reach that present state, and the gap between our past light cone and it is a **first-class ontological fact of the theory.** A relativistic frame can deflect the worry as ill-posed (no globally-preferred "now"); Synchronism *cannot* — its own ontology makes the unseen present real and the strobe-blindness a structural feature the program is obligated to carry.

## The steelman, and the residue that survives it

**Steelman (the defender's real move):** cosmology does not claim to photograph the present. It claims a *dynamical law*, and a strobe sample spread over billions of years is precisely the test bed for the law's **time-evolution** — nearby and deep-field samples probe the equation at different emission epochs, and their mutual consistency under one small parameter set is the evidence. "We don't see now" is not the own it first appears: the model isn't trying to.

**Surviving residue (the sharp form):** dynamical consistency tests the law *given its own assumed stationary form.* A strobe sample cannot distinguish "one universal law integrated over time" from "a sequence of states merely fittable by a smooth law" — genuine **underdetermination**. And it is structurally blind to anything not yet propagated into our light cone: **non-stationarity, or recent/local new physics in the actual present.** Honest ceiling: *the data constrains our past light cone under an assumed-stationary dynamics — nothing more.*
