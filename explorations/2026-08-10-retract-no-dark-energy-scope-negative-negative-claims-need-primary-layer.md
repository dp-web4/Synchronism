PREDICTIONS / STATUS) and never touched `Research/`. A compilation document can establish that something IS
present; it can **never** establish that something is ABSENT — absence lives in the primary derivation layer or
nowhere. New standing rule (adopted):

> **A negative existence claim ("the framework has no X", "nothing sources Y") must be verified against the
> PRIMARY derivation layer (`Research/`, `manuscripts/`, `explorations/`), never against compilation docs.**
